About Cosmic Harmony

The major/minor harmonic astronomy diagrams are inspired by Randy Halberstadt's Metaphors for the Musician, Chapter 22, “Harmonic Astronomy.” This browser implementation is a new interactive adaptation, not a reproduction of his diagram. Blues mode is an original experimental extension.

Use Sun or planet to branch to any planet or moon; from distant moons proceed to the close moons of that planet; from close moons resolve to their destination or another chord sharing the destination root. The ♭II7 (×) alternatives are chromatic options.

Cosmic Harmony receives local MIDI from d3SRT when launched as a companion window. Saved paths are stored in this browser; export a backup if needed.
